## Acceptance criteria

1. Private build configuration selects the approved voice at startup for shared app/widget/guide/practice speech and cold Live Activity replies. No new in-app choice or legacy preference controls this demo selection. Non-demo remains local.
2. Greeting, acknowledgement, session-only and ordinary replies actually use the configured cloud voice in demo mode. Match approved audition model/settings. Avoid unsolicited cloud prefetch and persistent caching of demo audio. Stop/timeouts discard late audio.
3. Missing configuration, offline/HTTP/audio failure produces truthful, explicitly identified Apple recovery, never a silent claim that Apple speech is the clone.
4. Keep credentials, identifiers and audio private and out of Git. Add meaningful regressions, run available checks, report cloud dataflow and exact Mac/device gates. Commit and normal-push completed code/bookkeeping without merging main.
