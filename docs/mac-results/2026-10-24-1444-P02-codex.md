## Top 5 problems for the demo

1. **Microphone errors can stop the conversation with no typed fallback.** Repeated retry text appears while the UI may still say listening. The exact scripted journey could not be completed, even after the owner left the machine idle. The environment may contribute. [Screenshot](P02-screens/88-promax-quiet-retry-error.png)
2. **Maximum Larger Text makes onboarding inaccessible.** Both decision buttons leave the visible screen and scrolling does not reveal them. Older users who need the largest text cannot proceed normally. [Screenshot](P02-screens/80-promax-maxtext-explainer-clipped.png)
3. **Guidance tells the user to act on the wrong state.** “Type in Central” appears after Central is filled, while destination is highlighted. Other captions refer to From/To labels that do not appear on the controls. [Screenshot](P02-screens/57-promax-guidance-origin-filled.png)
4. **Simulated results look like real transport advice.** A malformed transcript becomes a station, a fixed city label is used, and a 21:33 service is described as leaving shortly at 14:38. Add an unmistakable demo indication before showing this to users. [Station screenshot](P02-screens/59-promax-guidance-result.png), [timetable screenshot](P02-screens/61-promax-guidance-final.png)
5. **The first guidance caption covers navigation and lacks a distinct target highlight.** Tab labels remain visible below the caption, but icons are obscured and Journey Planner is not outlined. [Screenshot](P02-screens/55-promax-guidance-home.png)

Additional issues: no observed progress indicator in the captured guided steps; light appearance persists under system dark mode; cancel retains stale error text. Exact confirmation wording, real PTV integration, maximum-text downstream screens and audible Mac speaker output remain unverified.
