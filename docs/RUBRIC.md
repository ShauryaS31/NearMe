# Judging rubric and tracks

All four criteria apply to every team, each 1 to 5, max 20. Winners announced Sunday 16:00.

## Tracks (pick one or more; winners get 2x M6 Mac mini for the team; every track needs a working on-device demo)
- Enterprise: AI that solves a real business problem (internal tools, productivity, workflows companies would pay to fix).
- Community Impact: on-device AI that makes a real difference for people and communities.

NearMe submits to **Community (primary)** and **Enterprise** (IT helpdesk angle).

## Criteria
- **Core ML / Core AI / Apple Intelligence:** 1 chat wrapper | 2 basic prompting | 3 model plus one shallow extra | 4 model plus real integration with sensible data handling | 5 model plus data retrieval plus PII-aware handling, end-to-end function.
- **Execution and Craft:** 1 barely functional | 2 rough | 3 solid | 4 polished, considered | 5 near shippable, delightful.
- **Enterprise Value (security, cost, architecture):** 1 none | 2 one pillar superficially | 3 one pillar reasonably | 4 two pillars strongly | 5 all three credibly.
- **Community and Social Impact:** 1 none | 2 vague | 3 plausible | 4 strong, clear beneficiary group | 5 compelling, well executed, meaningful.
